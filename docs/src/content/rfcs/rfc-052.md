## Context

Until now one answerer served an execution: the cell that runs it ([RFC-048](/rfcs/rfc-048)). That cell answers an open term from its own chronicle, or says nothing. Some facts are kept by another actor, though. Art. 2 lid 2 of the Regeling gehandicaptenparkeerkaart lets the examination be left out when "aan de aanvrager eerder een gehandicaptenparkeerkaart is verstrekt", and the card may have come from another municipality or from the CBR (BABW art. 49 lid 3). The medical examination itself is the GGD's, or that of an expert the municipality designates (Regeling art. 3 lid 1). The municipality that decides holds neither fact.

The position paper calls the combining of answers from several cells synthesis. "Waar reductie maximaal de reductie binnen één cel kan bieden, is er vaak meer analyse nodig. Dat betekent het betrekken van meerdere lexostatussen uit meerdere cellen en het combineren, vergelijken en beoordelen daarvan." It places synthesis with the consumer: "Synthese gebeurt bij de afnemer, niet bij de bron, wat de autonomie van registrerende partijen waarborgt." [RFC-022](/rfcs/rfc-022) §4.1 takes that over, and the open questions of [RFC-050](/rfcs/rfc-050) asked how a second answerer is addressed.

This RFC answers that question with what the law already says: who is competent.

## Decision

Synthesis is the step in which the process gathers, for one open term, the answers of every cell the law makes competent for it, and makes one value of them with a rule from the law. The process is the engine executing the article the deciding cell decides on. Each cell reduces only its own chronicle. A reduction asks only its own cell.

1. **The reading law uses `source.regulation`.** A value another actor establishes is read as an output of the article that establishes it ([RFC-007](/rfcs/rfc-007)). That article declares the fact as an open term and gives it as an output. The law gains no word for cells or chronicles.
2. **Competence sits on the open term or the fact.** An open term and a `requires` entry with `fact` (schema v0.8.7) may each carry their own `competent_authority`, in the shape of [RFC-002](/rfcs/rfc-002): one named authority or a list. On the term it says who may answer it. Whether a parking card was issued before is for any college and for the CBR (BABW art. 49 lid 1 and 3), so that term names both. On the fact it says who establishes it: the medical examination of Regeling gehandicaptenparkeerkaart art. 3 lid 1 is the GGD's or the designated expert's.

   On the article, `competent_authority` keeps its meaning of RFC-002: who decides. It names one authority. BABW art. 49 names the college that issues the card, and the list of who may answer moves to the term. One article can establish several terms with different competent actors, and each term says which.
3. **The engine asks only competent answerers.** For an open term that names `competent_authority`, of an article a `source.regulation` read reaches, the engine asks every answerer of the call whose authority the term names: the running cell and the other cells of the sphere (`Call::sphere`). An `INSTANCE` matches the answerer's name, a `CATEGORY` its category, as exact text.

   Everywhere else only the running cell is asked, as before: at the top of an execution, in a call through `source.article` and for a term without `competent_authority`. The article's own `competent_authority` routes nothing.
4. **`combine` sits on the open term,** with the values and the code of FOREACH (`OR`, `AND`, `ADD`, `MIN`, `MAX`). `ONLY` is no rule for answers: one answer needs none, and two without a rule fail already, so the loader refuses it there. `combine` is refused too on a term that names no competent authorities, since only one answerer is asked for it. A `null` answer is silence and does not count. One answer is the value. Several answers without `combine` are an error that names the answerers. Without any answer the term falls back on its finding or its default, as now.
5. **An answer from a non-competent cell is an error.** The engine does not ask such a cell. The cell refuses, before each decision and for every cell of the sphere, a reduction that fills an open term whose `competent_authority` does not name it. It records a fact only when the fact's `competent_authority` names it, where the fact names one.
6. **A cell's identity is a small `cel.yaml`** with `name` and `category`. Identity cannot come from the law, so this is the one piece of cell configuration that stays. The engine matches an `INSTANCE` by name and a `CATEGORY` by category. In this proof of concept the law names categories for municipalities and the GGD, so a category stands for every cell of it; which instance is meant is open (see below).
7. **Replay stores only the day.** A decision keeps no chronicle position of another cell. On replay, every other cell answers from its chronolexograms recorded up to that day. That is the assumption of [RFC-050](/rfcs/rfc-050): "every source a decision reads can say what it knew on a given day."
8. **A decision is what its item lists.** What belongs to a decision is the `outputs` and `derived` of its item in `produces` ([RFC-047](/rfcs/rfc-047), [RFC-050](/rfcs/rfc-050)). Another output of the deciding article belongs to no item: the decision does not compute it, the cell does not record it with the decision, and a replay does not compare it. The engine computes only what is asked and what that depends on: for a decision its item's outputs and derived data, for a `source.regulation` read the output it names. A term routed to other cells is asked only when something reads it, so a fact may sit on the article that decides.

### The engine

The engine compares an authority from the law with the authority an answerer gives (`OpenTermAnswerer::authority`). It knows nothing of cells.

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Authority` | `name` and an optional `category`, as the answerer names itself |
| `Call::sphere` | The other answerers, beside `answerer` |
| Routing | A `source.regulation` read marks the article it evaluates; only for the open terms of that article does the engine route on competence. The answers of a read are cached apart from the same output read otherwise |
| Order | The competent answerers are asked in the order of their names, all of them, without short-circuit. Every `combine` value is independent of the order |
| `combine` | The aggregation code of FOREACH, shared (`combine_with`) |
| Trace | One node for the term, and under it one node per answerer that answered, silent ones included, each with its `answer` provenance. The term's node carries no `answer` of its own then; without routing it does, as before |
| `open_terms` | `FilledBy::Answer` for one answer, `FilledBy::Answers` for several combined |
| Load check | `combine` must fit the term's type: `OR` and `AND` a boolean, `ADD`, `MIN` and `MAX` a number or an amount. `ONLY`, and `combine` on a term that names no competent authorities, are refused. A list on an article, an empty list, a `#` reference on a term or a fact, and a competence on a `requires` entry without `fact` are refused as well |

A `#` reference stays possible on the article, where it says who decides (Wlz art. 2.1.1). A term or a fact names its authorities by name; a competence computed by the law waits for a case that needs it.

When no answerer of the call is competent, the trace says so ("No competent answerer in the sphere for ...") and the term falls back. A sphere in a proof of concept is never complete.

### Where each choice comes from

| Choice | Source |
|---|---|
| Synthesis happens in the process, never in a cell | The paper: "Synthese gebeurt bij de afnemer, niet bij de bron" |
| The reading law reads with `source.regulation`, without a new word | Owner's decision (20 October 2026) |
| Competence on the term or the fact, in the shape of RFC-002 | Owner's decision (20 October 2026), after the first version put a list on the article and mixed who decides with who may answer |
| On a fact, `competent_authority` names who establishes it | Owner's decision. RFC-022 §1.3 keeps `recording_actor` and `competent_authority` apart for an executogram. That still holds: the cell records, the law says who is competent, and where the cell establishes the fact itself the two coincide |
| The term that reads an examination names its own competence, equal to the fact's | Own choice. Deriving it from the fact would need the engine to link a term to a fact, and the term names the same authorities in one line |
| Routing only for an article reached through `source.regulation` | Own choice, recommended in the design. The deciding cell's own judgements and a decision called anew (`source.article`) stay with the running cell, and vakantieverlof does not change |
| `combine` on the open term, with the values of FOREACH | Owner's decision |
| One answer stays `FilledBy::Answer` | Own choice: a consumer that reads one answerer keeps working |
| Identity in `cel.yaml`, matched on the category | Owner's decision |
| A decision is what its item lists; the engine computes only what is asked | Owner's decision (20 October 2026), after RFC-047 and RFC-050 |
| Whether a card was issued before on Regeling art. 2 lid 2 | Own choice: the article that holds the words |
| Replay on the day | Owner's decision, on the assumption of RFC-050 |

## Why

### Benefits

- The law names a role, never a cell. A municipality, the CBR and a GGD answer because the law makes them competent.
- The scaling problem of RFC-050 goes away. The KvK would answer the type of registration once, on the establishing article of the Handelsregisterwet, and every law that needs it reads that output. One `implements` in the KvK cell, not one per asking law.
- The engine stays unaware of cells. It compares two names.
- Every source is visible in the trace, with who said what.

### Tradeoffs

- In the proof of concept a category matches every cell of it. A card of any college in the sphere counts, whether or not the applicant lived there.
- A replay asks the other cells as of the decision's day, so whatever they record later with a date up to that day enters the replay: a record made later that same day, or a record another cell dates back, since each chronicle keeps its own clock. A competent cell added to the sphere after the decision, or one removed, does the same.
- The replay compares the outcomes of the decision. Where such a change alters an outcome, the replay fails hard. Where it only changes who filled a term, the replay passes: a finding of the college can become another cell's answer, and what `Cell::findings` reports changes with it. Storing provenance with the decision would catch this; the owner chose to store only the day.
- A term that reads a fact, such as "an examination took place", names the same authorities as the fact. The two can drift apart, and nothing checks that they agree.

### Alternatives Considered

- **A new field `answered_from` on the reading term**, pointing at the establishing article. Rejected by the owner: no new word, no chronolexography in the law.
- **A list of answers as a value, with a FOREACH in the law.** The law would see cells, and an open term cannot be a list.
- **A composite answerer in `packages/cel`.** The rule and the competence would sit in Rust, and the engine would see one source.
- **A cell id in `source.regulation`**, as RFC-022 §4.2 sketched. The law would name a cell instead of a role.

## Open questions

- **Which instance.** The law often names a category and points at the instance through a fact. The college competent under BABW art. 49 lid 1 is that of the municipality where the applicant is registered as a resident; the GGD of art. 3 is the one the municipality designates. Both follow from a lexostatus of another cell (the BRP, the municipality's designation). RFC-002 tracks the same question in [#1029](https://github.com/NederlandseDigitaleDienst/regelrecht/issues/1029).
- **Agree or fail.** A rule that requires all answers to be equal (`SAME`) is left for the DUO case of [RFC-051](/rfcs/rfc-051).
- **A `#` reference on a term.** A competence the law computes through an output waits for a case that needs it.

## Implementation

- Schema v0.8.8: `competent_authority` on an open term and on a `requires` entry with `fact`, one named authority or a list; `combine` on an open term.
- `packages/law-model`: `CompetentAuthority::List`, `NamedAuthority`, `OpenTerm::competent_authority`, `OpenTerm::combine`, `ArticleRequirement::competent_authority`.
- `packages/engine`: `Authority` and `OpenTermAnswerer::authority`, `Call::sphere`, the routing and the combining in `ask_answerers` (`service.rs`), `combine_with` (`operations.rs`), `FilledBy::Answers`, `load_check::reject_misplaced_competence`. Tests in `packages/engine/tests/synthesis.rs`.
- `packages/cel`: `cel.yaml` read by `identity::read` when a cell opens, `Cell::authority`, `reading::refuse_incompetent` among the checks before each decision, for the deciding cell and each cell of the sphere, the refusal in `record_executogram` of a fact whose `competent_authority` does not name the cell, and the sphere as an argument of `record_submission`, `decide`, `explain`, `explain_answer` and `findings`. A cell of the sphere answers from its chronolexograms recorded up to the day of the decision (`Cell::known_on`). `reading::load` loads the reductions of all cells of a decision. Inside a reduction the cell asks only itself. Tests in `packages/cel/tests/synthese.rs`.
- Engine: `load_check::reject_ambiguous_produces` lets an output beside a decision that lists its own belong to no item. Tests in `packages/engine/tests/synthesis.rs` and `packages/cel/tests/synthese.rs`: such an output is not recorded with the decision, and its routed term is asked only when read.
- Corpus: BABW art. 49 names the college as the one who decides. `aanvrager_eerder_gehandicaptenparkeerkaart_verstrekt` on Regeling art. 2 lid 2 names every college and the CBR in its `competent_authority`, with `combine: OR`. On Regeling art. 3 the fact `geneeskundig_onderzoek_verricht` and the open term `geneeskundig_onderzoek_heeft_plaatsgehad` both name the GGD and the designated expert. Art. 2 reads the examination's term through an output of art. 3. The cells `ggd_wilgendaal` (the examination, moved from the municipality), `gemeente_oostmeer` (fictitious) and `cbr`, each with `cel.yaml` and its own reduction. Tests in `packages/cel/tests/parkeerkaart.rs`.
- The PoC app: `andere_cellen` in the case file, each cell with what it records before the first scenario; every step takes them along as the sphere; a tab where another cell records an instance of a fact; the chronicles of all cells. See [the PoC portal](/components/poc-portal).

Whether a card was issued before sits on Regeling art. 2 lid 2, not on BABW art. 49 as the design proposed. Lid 2 holds the words ("aan de aanvrager eerder een gehandicaptenparkeerkaart is verstrekt") and asks them of "de verstrekkende instantie"; art. 49 says who issues a card but never speaks of an earlier one. The term is routed because BABW art. 49 reaches art. 2 through `source.regulation`.
