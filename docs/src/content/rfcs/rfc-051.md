## Context

Until now a cell recorded two things: a submission, and the decision on one ([RFC-046](/rfcs/rfc-046), [RFC-047](/rfcs/rfc-047)). Art. 11, onder f, of the Leerplichtwet 1969 needs more. The leave asked under art. 13a must fall "buiten de schoolvakanties". Up to this RFC the head judged that per application (`decided_per_case_by`). Which days are school holidays is no judgement, though. It follows from three things:

- the Regeling vaststelling schoolvakanties 2025–2030 (BWBR0049811), made under art. 15, second lid, of the Wet op het primair onderwijs. It fixes the Christmas and May holidays for every school (art. 5), and the summer holiday per region (art. 6). The region follows from the school's place of establishment (art. 2 and 3);
- where the school is established, which is a fact about the school;
- the autumn and spring holidays, which the school fixes itself.

None of these is a submission. The school fixes its holidays of its own accord, and nobody applies for the place where it is established. This RFC adds the two recordings that were missing and lets a reduction read them.

## Decision

1. **A decision taken ex officio.** `Cell::decide_ex_officio(service, law, article, supplied, on)` takes the decision of an article that no submission asks for. It runs the article once for the outputs of its decision item and appends one chronolexogram without `refers_to`. Its `kind` is the legal character, its fields are the article's parameters.
2. **An executogram.** `Cell::record_executogram(service, stream, supplied, on)` records a fact of a stream the cell keeps. No regulation runs for it. The chronolexogram has `kind: EXECUTOGRAM`, the stream's `$id` under `stream`, and the stream's ground as `grondslag`.
3. **A stream is declared with the cell.** `corpus/cells/<cell>/executogrammen/<id>.yaml` names the stream (`$id`), the article on whose ground the fact is recorded (`grondslag`) and the fields, with their types. The cell reads its streams when it opens.
4. **A reduction reads both.** `source: {decisions: <regulation>#<article>}` now also gives the decisions taken ex officio. `source: {executograms: <stream>}` (schema v0.8.6) gives one row per executogram of the stream.
5. **`combine: ONLY`** (schema v0.8.6) gives the one result a FOREACH filter leaves: null when it leaves none, an error when it leaves more than one. A reduction picks the record in force on a day with it.
6. **The cell answers inside its own reductions.** When a reduction calls rulework that reads an open term the cell answers, the cell answers it there too.

Neither recording path names an Awb article, and both record nothing when a value is malformed.

### 1. A decision taken ex officio

What the cell refuses, so that what it records is whole:

- an article that takes no decision (`produces` with a legal character);
- an article whose decision a submission asks for (`requests`). That decision is taken on the submission, with `decide`;
- an article that follows a procedure (one with a stage `BESLUIT`). The cell runs the stages of a procedure only for a decision on a submission;
- a decision with an output or a parameter of type `decision`. The cell fills a decision only from a submission that refers to one;
- a name the article does not declare as a parameter, a value of the wrong type, and a parameter that cannot be absent and is left out. There is no submission whose incompleteness a law speaks of (Awb 4:5 speaks of an application), so the cell records a decision whole or not at all.

The cell answers the engine for the terms its reductions fill, as in any decision (RFC-048 §3).

### 2. An executogram and its stream

```yaml
# corpus/cells/obs_de_klimop/executogrammen/vestiging.yaml
$id: vestiging
grondslag: regeling_vaststelling_schoolvakanties_20252030#2
fields:
  - {name: gemeente, type: string}
  - {name: provincie, type: string, nullable: true}
  - {name: deel_van_gemeente, type: string, nullable: true}
  - {name: sinds, type: date}
```

A field is a string, a number, a boolean or a date, and none is named `id`: the row gives the executogram's id under that name. The cell refuses an unknown key, a file not named after the `$id`, and any other entry in `executogrammen/` that is not hidden. When it opens, it refuses a reduction that reads a stream it does not keep, and an executogram in its chronicle of such a stream. When it records, it refuses a ground that has no version in force that day, and the same malformed values it refuses for a decision taken ex officio (§1).

The stream is no rulework and has no `$schema`. `just validate` therefore checks only the reductions of a cell (`corpus/cells/*/reducties`), and the cell checks its streams itself.

Who records stays apart from what is recorded. The school is the cell (`recording_actor`). Its place is a fact on a time axis: "vestiging op Utrecht sinds 1 augustus 2020". A move is a new executogram, and the earlier one stays.

### 3. The school holidays of vakantieverlof

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| Regeling art. 2 | Declares the place of establishment as three open terms (`gemeente_van_vestiging`, `provincie_van_vestiging`, `deel_van_gemeente_van_vestiging`) and gives `regio_van_school`. |
| Regeling art. 3 | Gives the region of a municipality: all provinces, and every municipality, former municipality and kern the article names. |
| Regeling art. 5 and 6 | Give the Christmas and May holidays of a school year, and the summer holiday of a year and a region. |
| School policy (fictitious) | `fictief_beleid_vakantierooster_obs_de_klimop`: the bevoegd gezag fixes an autumn and a spring holiday per school year. The cell records each fixing ex officio. |
| Stream `vestiging` | Where the school is established, since a day. |
| `reductie_plaats_van_vestiging` | Fills the three terms of art. 2 from the executogram with the latest `sinds` on or before the day of the decision (`combine: ONLY`). |
| `reductie_verlof_valt_buiten_schoolvakanties` | Fills `verlof_valt_buiten_schoolvakanties` of art. 13a. It asks the regeling for the region and the periods, takes the school's fixing for the school year of the first leave day, and says whether a leave day falls in one of the six holidays. |

The term of art. 13a is unchanged: required, `decided_per_case_by` the head, no default. In the school's cell the reduction answers it, so leave asked during a school holiday is refused without anyone judging it. When the cell does not know a holiday (the regeling fixes none for the region or the year, or the school fixed no holidays for the school year), its answer is null. The head decides the term per case, so the engine takes a null answer as silence (RFC-048), and the head judges then. Without a finding the decision fails.

### Where each choice comes from

| Choice | Source |
|---|---|
| The Christmas, May and summer holidays, and the region from the place of establishment | The law: the regeling, art. 2, 3, 5 and 6, under WPO art. 15 lid 2 |
| The fixing of the autumn and spring holidays is the school's own policy | Own choice. No article grants the bevoegd gezag this power in so many words. WMS art. 11 lid 1 onder l presumes it: the MR may advise on "elk door het bevoegd gezag te nemen besluit" on the "regeling van de vakantie". That is advice, not consent, and it is not modeled |
| The fixing is a decretogram | The paper: a decretogram records "een concreet besluit of beschikking bij de toepassing/werking van wetgeving". It need not be an Awb besluit |
| Its legal character `BESLUIT_VAN_ALGEMENE_STREKKING`, `ANDERE_HANDELING` | Own choice. It holds for every pupil of the school, and no Awb hook fires on it. Whether it is a besluit in the sense of the Awb is left open |
| The place of establishment is an executogram | Owner's decision (18 October 2026). The paper defines an executogram as "de vastlegging van daadwerkelijke levering of afhandeling" of a zaak or dienst. A place of establishment fits that loosely; the paper calls its three types a first enumeration |
| Streams live in the cell's directory | Own choice. RFC-022 §1.3 put them in a top-level `chronicles/`. Reductions already live with the cell (RFC-048), and a stream is read only by them |
| The region on the day of the decision, from the latest `sinds` | Own choice |
| The school year of the leave is that of the first leave day | Own choice, as in RFC-048 §2 |
| Leave that has one day in a holiday does not fall outside the school holidays | Own choice: art. 11, onder f, says "slechts buiten de schoolvakanties" and does not split a request |
| Gemeente, provincie and deel as three open terms of art. 2 | Own choice. Art. 3 divides by province and municipality, and for four municipalities by a former municipality or a kern |
| The open bodies of Caribisch Nederland in the term for the municipality | Own choice |
| A municipality that art. 3 does not name is in no region | The law. West Betuwe (formed in 2019) is not named; art. 4 covers mergers after publication only |
| Art. 6 keyed by calendar year | The law: "voor de jaren 2026, 2027, 2028, 2029 en 2030" |
| A null answer leaves the term to the head | RFC-048 and the engine: a null answer is silent where the term has a default or is decided per case |

## Why

### Benefits

- Leave in a school holiday is refused from what the school recorded and from the law. The trace of the decision shows one node, "answered by obs_de_klimop", and `Cell::explain_answer` gives the reasoning, down to the place of establishment.
- Both recording paths are generic. A municipality that fixes something of its own accord, or records where a person lives, uses the same calls.
- A move changes the region from its day, and the chronicle keeps both places.
- The engine stays unaware of cells. It gained one source key and one way to combine.

### Tradeoffs

- The region is that of the school on the day of the decision, not on the leave days. A school that moves to regio Zuid on 1 May 2027 has leave for July 2027 judged against its old region when the head decides in April, and against the new one in May. The holidays that matter are those of the leave. Taking the region on the first leave day would need art. 2 of the regeling to take a day, and its text names none. `schoolvakanties.rs` pins the current behavior.
- `Cell::explain`, `Cell::findings` and `Cell::explain_answer` replay a decision on a submission. A decision taken ex officio has none, and they refuse it.
- A reduction whose rulework asks its own answer again is refused (`CircularReference`), so a cycle fails the decision instead of running without end. The guard refuses any nested ask of the same term, also for another day or other parameters.
- A stream is a second kind of file in a cell, with no JSON schema of its own. The cell's own parser is its only check, when it records and when it opens the chronicle.
- An ex officio decision runs no procedure. An ex officio beschikking, with its announcement and objection period, is refused, not built.
- A second fixing of the holidays for the same school year does not replace the first. `combine: ONLY` refuses the two, so the decision fails. Revoking or replacing a decretogram taken ex officio is not modeled.
- The regeling is modeled for one establishment per school. A school with establishments in several regions (art. 2, third sentence) is not.
- Art. 7 of the regeling is not modeled: the extension of the summer holiday by up to two days by the bevoegd gezag (lid 1), the other deviations (lid 2 to 5), and the five weeks of the Wadden islands (lid 6). Neither are the exemption by the minister (art. 8) or free days that are no holiday.
- The question to the cell runs in its own execution. A failure inside it, such as two establishments since the same day, surfaces as an error of the cell, not of art. 13a.

### Alternatives Considered

- **The vestiging as a field of the cell's identity.** Rejected: identity and place are different facts, and a place changes over time.
- **The holidays as a fictitious policy without a chronicle.** Rejected by the owner (18 October 2026). A school fixes its holidays as a decision with a day, and a reduction should read that decision as it reads others.
- **Streams in a top-level `chronicles/`, as RFC-022 §1.3 has it.** Not taken here, see the table above.
- **Indexing an array (`$rij.0`) instead of `combine: ONLY`.** The engine supports the index, the schema does not. An index takes the first of two records silently, while `ONLY` refuses two.

## Implementation

- Schema v0.8.6: `source.executograms`, only in a `REDUCTIE` and alone in its `source`; `combine: ONLY`.
- Engine: `Source::executograms` and `CombineOp::Only` in `law-model`; `load_check::chronicle_read` for both chronicle sources; `combine_only` in `operations.rs`; the type checker and the unit inference take the body's type and unit.
- `packages/cel`: `stream.rs` (`Stream`, loading `executogrammen/`), `Gram::stream`, `Cell::record_executogram`, `Cell::decide_ex_officio`, `Cell::streams`, `reading::executogram_rows`, `reading::refuse_unknown_streams`; `reading::rows` no longer requires `refers_to`; the `Reader` answers inside its own reductions.
- Corpus: the regeling with art. 2, 3, 5 and 6 executable and BDD scenarios; the school's fictitious holiday policy; two outputs added to the school-year policy (`eerste_jaar_schooljaar`, `laatste_jaar_schooljaar`); the stream and the two reductions of OBS De Klimop.
- Not in the corpus: the Wet op het primair onderwijs. Its harvested text is over the 500 KB a file may have, so the regeling names it as `legal_basis` without it.
- Tests: `packages/cel/tests/schoolvakanties.rs` (Christmas, region, the school's own autumn holiday, a move) and `packages/cel/tests/ex_officio.rs` (both recording paths).

## Open questions

- **Another cell answers where the school is.** DUO keeps the register of institutions (RIO). A DUO cell could answer the place of establishment instead of the school's own executogram. That needs more than one answerer in an execution, and a choice of which one answers a term. Not built.
- **Replacing a decision taken ex officio.** A school that changes its holidays now records a second fixing that conflicts with the first.
- **A stream of more than one cell.** RFC-022 §1.3 has a stream name the chronicle it joins. Here a stream belongs to one cell.
