## Context

Until now a cell recorded two things: a submission, and the decision on one ([RFC-046](/rfcs/rfc-046), [RFC-047](/rfcs/rfc-047)). Art. 11, onder f, of the Leerplichtwet 1969 needs more. The leave asked under art. 13a must fall "buiten de schoolvakanties". Up to this RFC the head judged that per application (`decided_per_case_by`). Which days are school holidays is no judgement, though. It follows from three things:

- the Regeling vaststelling schoolvakanties 2025–2030 (BWBR0049811), made under art. 15, second lid, of the Wet op het primair onderwijs. It fixes the Christmas and May holidays for every school (art. 5), and the summer holiday per region (art. 6). The region follows from the school's place of establishment (art. 2 and 3);
- where the school is established, which is a fact about the school;
- the autumn and spring holidays, which the school fixes itself.

None of these is a submission. Nobody applies for the place where a school is established, and the school sets its own holidays as a rule, the way a school guide does. This RFC adds the recording that was missing, the executogram, and lets a reduction read it next to the law and the school's policy.

## Decision

1. **An executogram.** `Cell::record_executogram(service, stream, supplied, on)` records a fact of a stream the cell keeps. No regulation runs for it. The chronolexogram has `kind: EXECUTOGRAM`, the stream's `$id` under `stream`, and the stream's ground as `grondslag`.
2. **A stream is declared with the cell.** `corpus/cells/<cell>/executogrammen/<id>.yaml` names the stream (`$id`), the article on whose ground the fact is recorded (`grondslag`) and the fields, with their types. The cell reads its streams when it opens.
3. **A reduction reads a stream.** `source: {executograms: <stream>}` (schema v0.8.6) gives one row per executogram of the stream.
4. **The school's own holidays are policy.** The school's fictitious policy holds its autumn and spring holidays as outputs, with one version per school year from 1 August (`valid_from`). The holiday reduction reads them through `source.regulation`, as it reads the regeling.
5. **`combine: ONLY`** (schema v0.8.6) gives the one result a FOREACH filter leaves: null when it leaves none, an error when it leaves more than one. A reduction picks the record in force on a day with it.
6. **The cell answers inside its own reductions.** When a reduction calls rulework that reads an open term the cell answers, the cell answers it there too. A reduction asked there takes a parameter it declares from the article that asks it, or else from the nearest question around it, such as the leave days of art. 13a.
7. **The region is that of the leave days.** The school's vestiging, and so its region, is the one in force on the first and the last leave day. When the school moves within the leave, the decision fails.
8. **A report received is an executogram too.** For the parking card, the municipality records that it received the report of the medical examination (Regeling gehandicaptenparkeerkaart, art. 2 and 3), in a stream `geneeskundig_onderzoek` of its own cell. The examination is the GGD's; the receipt is the municipality's fact.

Recording an executogram names no Awb article, and records nothing when a value is malformed.

### 1. An executogram and its stream

```yaml
# corpus/cells/obs_de_klimop/executogrammen/vestiging.yaml
$id: vestiging
grondslag: regeling_vaststelling_schoolvakanties_20252030#2
fields:
  - {name: gemeente, type: string}
  - {name: provincie, type: string, nullable: true}
  - {name: deel_van_gemeente, type: string, nullable: true}
  - {name: sinds, type: date}
```

A field is a string, a number, a boolean or a date, and none is named `id`: the row gives the executogram's id under that name. The cell refuses an unknown key, a file not named after the `$id`, and any other entry in `executogrammen/` that is not hidden. When it opens, it refuses a reduction that reads a stream it does not keep, and an executogram in its chronicle of such a stream. When it records, it refuses a ground that has no version in force that day, a name the stream does not declare, a value of the wrong type, and a field that cannot be absent and is left out. No law speaks of an incomplete executogram (Awb 4:5 speaks of an application), so the cell records one whole or not at all.

The stream is no rulework and has no `$schema`. `just validate` therefore checks only the reductions of a cell (`corpus/cells/*/reducties`), and the cell checks its streams itself.

Who records stays apart from what is recorded. The school is the cell (`recording_actor`). Its place is a fact on a time axis: "vestiging op Utrecht sinds 1 augustus 2020". A move is a new executogram, and the earlier one stays.

### 2. The school holidays of vakantieverlof

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| Regeling art. 2 | Declares the place of establishment as three open terms (`gemeente_van_vestiging`, `provincie_van_vestiging`, `deel_van_gemeente_van_vestiging`) and gives `regio_van_school`. |
| Regeling art. 3 | Gives the region of a municipality: all provinces, and every municipality, former municipality and kern the article names. |
| Regeling art. 5 and 6 | Give the Christmas and May holidays of a school year, and the summer holiday of a year and a region. |
| School policy (fictitious) | `fictief_beleid_vakantierooster_obs_de_klimop`: the school's autumn and spring holidays per school year. The version of 1 August 2026 holds those of 2026–2027 and nothing for another year. |
| Stream `vestiging` | Where the school is established, since a day. |
| `reductie_plaats_van_vestiging` | Fills the three terms of art. 2 from the executogram in force on the first and the last leave day: the one with the latest `sinds` on or before that day. Two executograms, because the school moves within the leave, are refused (`combine: ONLY`). |
| `reductie_verlof_valt_buiten_schoolvakanties` | Fills `verlof_valt_buiten_schoolvakanties` of art. 13a. It asks the regeling for the region and its periods, and the school's policy for the autumn and spring holidays of the school year of the first leave day. It then says whether a leave day falls in one of the six holidays. |

The term of art. 13a is unchanged: required, `decided_per_case_by` the head, no default. In the school's cell the reduction answers it, so leave asked during a school holiday is refused without anyone judging it. When the cell does not know a holiday (the regeling fixes none for the region or the year, or the school's policy in force does not hold the school year), its answer is null. The head decides the term per case, so the engine takes a null answer as silence (RFC-048), and the head judges then. Without a finding the decision fails.

### 3. The medical examination of the parking card

Art. 2 lid 1 of the Regeling gehandicaptenparkeerkaart (BWBR0012625) reads: "Een gehandicaptenparkeerkaart wordt niet afgegeven alvorens een geneeskundig onderzoek heeft plaatsgehad met betrekking tot de handicap van de aanvrager." Art. 3 lid 1 says who examines for a municipality: the GGD, or with external advice a "door het gemeentelijk gezag aangewezen deskundige". The municipality does not examine. It learns of the examination when the report comes in, as the school learns of a court's judgment in the design for appeals.

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| Stream `geneeskundig_onderzoek` | The report received: whose examination (`bsn_aanvrager`), on which day (`datum_onderzoek`), and by whom (`verricht_door`). Its ground is the Regeling, art. 2. |
| Regeling art. 2 | Declares `geneeskundig_onderzoek_heeft_plaatsgehad` and `aanvrager_eerder_gehandicaptenparkeerkaart_verstrekt`, both required and decided per case by the college. Gives `gehandicaptenparkeerkaart_mag_worden_afgegeven`: lid 2 first, then lid 1. |
| `reductie_geneeskundig_onderzoek` | Fills the first term: true when the cell holds a report on the applicant of an examination on or after the date of the application and on or before the day of issue. Otherwise null. |
| `reductie_geldige_gehandicaptenparkeerkaart` | Also fills the second term, from the college's own decisions: true when it issued the applicant a card before that was not revoked. Otherwise null. |
| BABW art. 49 | Passes the date of the application (Awb 4:2, `dagtekening`) and three new judgements of the college to art. 2 (lid 2: whether it leaves the examination out, and the judgements under a and b), and issues a card only when art. 2 allows it. |

Lid 1 forbids issuing a card before the examination. It gives no ground to refuse one. Art. 49 asks art. 2 only for a card it would otherwise issue, so a refusal on the criteria or on the municipality's policy needs no report. When the card would be issued and no report is in the cell, the reduction is silent and the college has given no finding, so the engine fails the decision on the required term. The cell records nothing, and the college decides once the report is in.

Lid 2 says the examination "kan achterwege worden gelaten" for someone who had a card before, when the issuing body knows the applicant still qualifies (onder a) or the examining body judges so (onder b). The power and both judgements belong to the authorities, so they are open terms on art. 49, like the medical judgements. Whether a card was issued before is a fact, and the cell answers it from its own decisions. The cell knows only its own cards; a card of another municipality or of the CBR counts as well, so finding none, the cell says nothing. Lid 2 onder c (short stay, the CBR under art. 49 lid 3) and lid 3 (an institution, art. 49 lid 2) are not modeled, because those parts of art. 49 are not.

### Where each choice comes from

| Choice | Source |
|---|---|
| The Christmas, May and summer holidays, and the region from the place of establishment | The law: the regeling, art. 2, 3, 5 and 6, under WPO art. 15 lid 2 |
| The autumn and spring holidays are the school's own policy | Own choice. No article grants the bevoegd gezag this power in so many words. WMS art. 11 lid 1 onder l presumes it: the MR may advise on "elk door het bevoegd gezag te nemen besluit" on the "regeling van de vakantie". That is advice, not consent, and it is not modeled |
| The holidays are policy, not a decision in the chronicle | Owner's decision (19 October 2026). They are a rule of the school, as in a school guide, not an event in a process |
| One version of the policy per school year | Own choice, after that decision: a school year gets its version from 1 August (`valid_from`) |
| The place of establishment is an executogram | Owner's decision (18 October 2026). The paper defines an executogram as "de vastlegging van daadwerkelijke levering of afhandeling" of a zaak or dienst. A place of establishment fits that loosely; the paper calls its three types a first enumeration |
| Streams live in the cell's directory | Own choice. RFC-022 §1.3 put them in a top-level `chronicles/`. Reductions already live with the cell (RFC-048), and a stream is read only by them |
| The region on the leave days, not on the day of the decision | Owner's decision (18 October 2026): what counts is whether the leave falls in the school's holidays |
| A move within the leave fails the decision, rather than judging each day | Own choice. The regeling gives a school one region (art. 2), so a leave in two regions has no answer in it. Judging each day would need the regeling per leave day, which a reduction cannot call per element |
| The leave days reach the reduction for the vestiging from the question around it | Own choice. Art. 2 of the regeling names no day; the cell passes on what art. 13a asked |
| The school year of the leave is that of the first leave day | Own choice, as in RFC-048 §2 |
| Leave that has one day in a holiday does not fall outside the school holidays | Own choice: art. 11, onder f, says "slechts buiten de schoolvakanties" and does not split a request |
| Gemeente, provincie and deel as three open terms of art. 2 | Own choice. Art. 3 divides by province and municipality, and for four municipalities by a former municipality or a kern |
| The open bodies of Caribisch Nederland in the term for the municipality | Own choice |
| A municipality that art. 3 does not name is in no region | The law. West Betuwe (formed in 2019) is not named; art. 4 covers mergers after publication only |
| Art. 6 keyed by calendar year | The law: "voor de jaren 2026, 2027, 2028, 2029 en 2030" |
| A null answer leaves the term to the head | RFC-048 and the engine: a null answer is silent where the term has a default or is decided per case |
| The municipality records the receipt of the report, not the examination | Owner's decision (20 October 2026), as for the court's judgment in the appeal design. The examination is the GGD's (art. 3 lid 1). A GGD cell would need answers across cells, which is not built |
| Without a known examination the decision fails, rather than refusing the card | The law: "wordt niet afgegeven alvorens" orders the steps and names no refusal. A refusal on another ground does not wait |
| An examination counts for an application from its date (Awb 4:2 lid 1 onder b) up to the day of issue | Own choice. Art. 2 lid 1 ties the examination to no application. Lid 2 onder a and b would mean nothing if the examination for an earlier card still counted. The decision does not know the day of receipt, so the date the applicant wrote on the application stands in for it |
| The power and the judgements of lid 2 as open terms on art. 49, not on the Regeling | Own choice, for the reason the medical judgements sit there: the cell derives the inputs of a decision from the deciding article only. Not required: a college that gives none does not leave the examination out |
| The judgement under b is given by the college | Own choice. The law gives it to the examining body; here the college passes it on as received |
| Lid 2 before lid 1 | Own choice: a college that leaves the examination out where it may is not asked whether one took place |

## Why

### Benefits

- Leave in a school holiday is refused from what the school recorded and from the law. The trace of the decision shows one node, "answered by obs_de_klimop", and `Cell::explain_answer` gives the reasoning, down to the place of establishment.
- The recording path is generic. A municipality that records where a person lives uses the same call.
- A move changes the region from its day, and the chronicle keeps both places.
- The engine stays unaware of cells. It gained one source key and one way to combine.

### Tradeoffs

- The reduction for the vestiging needs the leave days. Asked outside a question that has them, it gives no vestiging, so art. 2 gives no region.
- Leave during which the school moves cannot be decided in the cell. The head cannot decide it either, because the cell's error ends the decision.
- A reduction whose rulework asks its own answer again is refused (`CircularReference`), so a cycle fails the decision instead of running without end. The guard refuses any nested ask of the same term, also for another day or other parameters.
- A stream is a second kind of file in a cell, with no JSON schema of its own. The cell's own parser is its only check, when it records and when it opens the chronicle.
- The engine picks the version of the school's policy by the day of the decision, not by the leave days. Leave in the next school year, decided before 1 August, meets the current version, which does not hold that year, so the head judges.
- The regeling is modeled for one establishment per school. A school with establishments in several regions (art. 2, third sentence) is not.
- Art. 7 of the regeling is not modeled: the extension of the summer holiday by up to two days by the bevoegd gezag (lid 1), the other deviations (lid 2 to 5), and the five weeks of the Wadden islands (lid 6). Neither are the exemption by the minister (art. 8) or free days that are no holiday.
- The question to the cell runs in its own execution. A failure inside it, such as two establishments since the same day, surfaces as an error of the cell, not of art. 13a.
- A parking card that waits for its examination shows as a failed decision. The message names the term nobody answered, but it does not say that the law makes the college wait. Nothing records that the college tried.
- The date on the application is what the applicant wrote. An examination before the day of receipt but after that date counts.

### Alternatives Considered

- **The vestiging as a field of the cell's identity.** Rejected: identity and place are different facts, and a place changes over time.
- **The holidays as a decision taken ex officio.** An earlier draft of this RFC added `Cell::decide_ex_officio`, which recorded the school's fixing of its holidays as a decretogram without a submission. The owner dropped it (19 October 2026). The rooster existed only as fictitious policy, so recording it ex officio proved nothing the executogram does not already show.
- **Streams in a top-level `chronicles/`, as RFC-022 §1.3 has it.** Not taken here, see the table above.
- **Indexing an array (`$rij.0`) instead of `combine: ONLY`.** The engine supports the index, the schema does not. An index takes the first of two records silently, while `ONLY` refuses two.

## Implementation

- Schema v0.8.6: `source.executograms`, only in a `REDUCTIE` and alone in its `source`; `combine: ONLY`.
- Engine: `Source::executograms` and `CombineOp::Only` in `law-model`; `load_check::chronicle_read` for both chronicle sources; `combine_only` in `operations.rs`; the type checker and the unit inference take the body's type and unit.
- `packages/cel`: `stream.rs` (`Stream`, loading `executogrammen/`), `Gram::stream`, `Cell::record_executogram`, `Cell::streams`, `reading::executogram_rows`, `reading::refuse_unknown_streams`; the `Reader` answers inside its own reductions.
- Corpus: the regeling with art. 2, 3, 5 and 6 executable and BDD scenarios; the school's fictitious holiday policy, with the holidays of 2026–2027; two outputs added to the school-year policy (`eerste_jaar_schooljaar`, `laatste_jaar_schooljaar`); the stream and the two reductions of OBS De Klimop.
- Corpus: a selection from the Wet op het primair onderwijs, text only, as harvested. It holds art. 15, the regeling's `legal_basis`, and art. 1, 8, 9 and 10, which the Leerplichtwet 1969 and the regeling cite in their text.
- Tests: `packages/cel/tests/schoolvakanties.rs` (Christmas, region, the school's own autumn holiday, a move) and `packages/cel/tests/executograms.rs` (recording and reading back an executogram, and a reduction that asks its own answer).
- Parking card: the stream `geneeskundig_onderzoek` and `reductie_geneeskundig_onderzoek` of the municipality's cell; a second term in `reductie_geldige_gehandicaptenparkeerkaart`; the Regeling's art. 2 executable, with `scenarios/geneeskundig_onderzoek.feature`; art. 49 of the BABW with the new input, parameter and judgements, and its scenarios. Tests in `packages/cel/tests/parkeerkaart.rs`. No engine or cell code changed.
- The PoC app (`packages/poc-chronolexografie`, see [the PoC portal](/components/poc-portal)) records the school's vestiging whenever the cell of the vakantieverlof case starts, from `begin` in its case file, through `Cell::record_executogram`, dated 1 August 2026. Its form no longer asks the head whether the leave falls outside the school holidays. A seventh scenario plays leave in the Christmas holiday, refused without a judgement of the head; `kern.rs` plays it through the app's functions.

For the parking card, the app records the GGD's report on the example applicant from `begin`, dated 12 October 2026, so the existing scenarios still issue the card. Two scenarios are new: an applicant without a report gets no decision, and after five years the college leaves the examination out under lid 2 onder a. The app has no step that records a report, so it cannot play a card that follows once the report is in; the cell tests do.

## Open questions

- **Another cell answers where the school is.** DUO keeps the register of institutions (RIO). A DUO cell could answer the place of establishment instead of the school's own executogram. That needs more than one answerer in an execution, and a choice of which one answers a term. Not built.
- **Does the vocabulary of reductions belong in the law format?** Since RFC-048 the schema has gained `REDUCTIE` and `source.decisions`, then `implements` on a reduction (v0.8.5), and now `source.executograms` and `combine: ONLY` on a FOREACH (v0.8.6). These are constructs for a cell, not for law. They could move to an extension instead: a schema or profile of its own for reductions, or an extension namespace, so that the law format stays about law. Not decided (Tim, 19 October 2026).
- **The GGD's own cell.** The GGD could record the examination in its own chronicle and answer the municipality's question. That is the same step as the DUO cell above.
- **A stream of more than one cell.** RFC-022 §1.3 has a stream name the chronicle it joins. Here a stream belongs to one cell.
