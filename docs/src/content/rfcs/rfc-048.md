## Context

Some answers are in what an organization recorded earlier, not in the law:

- The head grants leave "slechts eenmaal ... per schooljaar" (Leerplichtwet 1969 art. 13a lid 2). Earlier grants are in the school's own decisions.
- Leave must fall "buiten de schoolvakanties" (art. 11 onder f). The holidays depend on where the school is established, a fact the school recorded.
- An examination may be skipped if "aan de aanvrager eerder een gehandicaptenparkeerkaart is verstrekt" (Regeling gehandicaptenparkeerkaart art. 2 lid 2). Another municipality or the CBR may have issued it.

The law leaves such terms to the authority and does not say how it reads its records back. The [position paper](https://chronolexografie.nl/position-paper/) does: "Reductie vindt altijd plaats ín de cel waar de betreffende chronolexogrammen zijn vastgelegd", and "Synthese gebeurt bij de afnemer, niet bij de bron". The law defines a cell's interface; the cell decides how it answers.

## Decision

How this works in practice: [Chronolexography](/concepts/chronolexography#answering-from-the-chronicle).

### 1. A reduction is rulework of its own kind

`regulatory_layer: REDUCTIE`, kept with its cell at `reducties/<id>/<valid_from>.yaml`. It fills one open term (`implements`).

```yaml
# corpus/cells/obs_de_klimop/reducties/reductie_verlof_al_verleend_in_schooljaar
implements:
  - {law: leerplichtwet_1969, article: 13a, open_term: verlof_al_verleend_in_schooljaar}
input:
  - name: besluiten
    type: array
    source:
      decisions: leerplichtwet_1969#13a
  - name: begin_schooljaar
    type: date
    source:
      regulation: fictief_beleid_schooljaargrenzen_obs_de_klimop
      parameters: {datum: $eerste_verlofdag}
```

- `source.decisions` (`<regulation>#<article>`): one row per decision, with fields, outputs and `id`.
- `source.facts` (`<regulation>#<article>#<fact>`): one row per recorded fact ([RFC-051](/rfcs/rfc-051)). The last `#` separates the fact.
- `combine: ONLY` on a `FOREACH`: the one result the filter leaves; null for none, an error for two.
- Only a `REDUCTIE` reads the chronicle. It has no `hooks`, `overrides`, `execution.produces` or `open_terms`.

### 2. The engine asks, the cell answers

A caller may give an execution an answerer (`OpenTermAnswerer`, in a `Call`). When an article reads an open term, at any depth, the engine asks it first. During a decision the cell is the answerer. It answers only terms its reductions in force implement, taking their parameters by name from the asking article or the question around it.

- The answer wins over policy and over a finding ([RFC-045](/rfcs/rfc-045) §5).
- On a term with a default or `decided_per_case_by`, null is silence and the term falls back. Elsewhere null is the value.
- No answer, no finding, no default: the execution fails. A missing answer never grants anything.
- The engine never runs a reduction itself; a `REDUCTIE` stays out of its implements index.

### 3. The directory says which terms are the cell's

A term any version of a reduction implements is the cell's. On a day no reduction in force fills it, the cell refuses the decision; decisions from before the first version still replay.

- On opening, the cell refuses a reduction that fills no term, a mismatched `$id` or file name, and any other entry under `reducties/`.
- Before each decision, it refuses a reduction that fills a term its article does not declare that day, or reads a fact its article does not require.
- `Cell::answers` lists what the cell can answer. Declaring that is a RegelRecht addition (RFC-022 §4.1), not the paper's.

### 4. The process combines the answers of competent cells

A decision may take other cells along: its sphere. For an open term with `competent_authority`, reached through `source.regulation`, the engine asks every answerer the term names: an `INSTANCE` by name, a `CATEGORY` by category. All are asked, in name order, without short-circuit.

| Answers | Value |
|---|---|
| none, or only nulls | falls back on the finding or the default; the trace says no one competent answered |
| one | that answer |
| several | merged with `combine` ([RFC-051](/rfcs/rfc-051) §3); without it, an error naming the answerers |

Elsewhere only the deciding cell is asked: at the top of an execution, in a `source.article` call, and for a term without `competent_authority`. A reduction asks only its own cell. Every cell refuses a reduction for a term whose `competent_authority` does not name it.

### 5. Who answered is in the result, why is with the cell

`ArticleResult.open_terms` reports `filled_by: {answer: ...}`, or `answers` when combined: the answerer, the reduction and its version, and how far it read the chronicle. The trace has one node for the term; a routed term holds a node per answerer, silent ones included. `Cell::explain_answer` reruns the reduction with tracing to show its reasoning. That trace is not stored.

### 6. Replay and reconsideration

On replay ([RFC-046](/rfcs/rfc-046)) the cell answers from its chronicle as it stood before the decision; sphere cells answer from what they recorded up to its day. Only that day is stored. When Awb 7:11 calls a decision anew, the cell answers in that call without the contested decision. Revoked decisions stay in the rows; the reduction leaves them out.

### Where each choice comes from

| Choice | Source |
|---|---|
| Reduction in the cell that holds the records | The paper |
| Synthesis in the asking process | The paper |
| Routing only through `source.regulation` | Own choice: the deciding cell's own judgements and calls anew stay with it |
| One answer stays `FilledBy::Answer` | Own choice: single-answer consumers keep working |
| Replay asks other cells as of the day; only the day is stored | Owner's decision |

## Why

### Benefits

- Law and policy say nothing about the chronicle. A rule such as the school year is written once, in policy.
- Anyone can answer: a cell, or a scenario with the authority's finding.
- A reduction is type-checked and traced like any article.
- The engine knows no cells; it asks an answerer and compares names.
- A value is answered once, on its establishing article. A Chamber of Commerce cell needs one `implements`, not one per asking law.

### Tradeoffs

- An authority's finding on a term the cell answers is set aside; the trace says so.
- A decision's trace shows who answered, not why. For why, ask the cell.

Open points: [Open Points](/concepts/chronolexography-open-points).

### Alternatives Considered

- **The reduction returns a list; policy computes.** Policy would hold logic that exists only for the chronicle.
- **A reduction language in cell configuration** (PR #1689, RFC-022 §4.1). A second language without type checker or trace.
- **The article asks the answer as a parameter** (first version). Lost in nested calls, invisible in the trace.
- **The engine runs the reduction as an implementation** (16–17 October 2026). Put the reduction's `FOREACH` in the decision's trace.
- **The cell drops revoked decisions from the rows.** The reduction would not see the whole picture.
- **A list of answers as a value, with `FOREACH` in the law.** The law would see cells.
- **A composite answerer in `packages/cel`.** Rule and competence in Rust; the engine would see one source.
