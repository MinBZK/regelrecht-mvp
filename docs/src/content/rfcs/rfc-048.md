## Decision

The reduction (RFC-022 §4.1, *chronolexoreductie*) is rulework of its own kind, `regulatory_layer: REDUCTIE`, kept with the cell. It finds the decisions that matter in the chronicle, calls law or policy for the logic, and returns the answer: the lexostatus. The decision takes that answer as an input by name.

1. **The law asks for the answer.** Leerplichtwet 1969 art. 13a gets the parameter `verlof_al_verleend_in_schooljaar` and refuses leave when it holds (lid 2: "slechts eenmaal ... per schooljaar"). It is no field of the application.
2. **The policy holds the rule, not the decisions.** `fictief_beleid_schooljaargrenzen_obs_de_klimop` says for a `datum` when its school year begins and ends. It reads no list and fills no open term.
3. **The reduction finds and asks.** `reductie_verlof_al_verleend_in_schooljaar` reads `besluiten` (`source: {decisions: leerplichtwet_1969#13a}`), asks the policy for the school year of `eerste_verlofdag`, and yields whether a decision granted the same child leave in it.
4. **The cell connects them by name.** When it decides, it runs each reduction whose output names an input of the decision that nothing else supplies. It passes the decision's own inputs as the reduction's parameters, and the answer to the decision, both by name.
5. **The decision records the answer** in `fields`, like a field of the application.

This replaces the sketch of RFC-022 §4.1, which put the rule of a reduction in cell configuration.

### 1. The contract: `source: {decisions}`

`source.decisions` names an article, `<regulation>#<article>`, and means "the decisions taken under this article".

- A row is one decision: its fields and the outputs of the decision item, by their own names. A name in both with different values is refused.
- Only a data source answers it, or the caller with a parameter of that name, as a test does. No source is an error, never an empty list.
- It stands alone: no `regulation`, `output`, `parameters` or `endpoint` beside it.
- Only a `REDUCTIE` may read it. A `REDUCTIE` has no `implements`, `hooks`, `overrides`, `execution.produces` or `open_terms`; the schema and the loader both refuse them.

A reduction may read other inputs like any rulework. The school's reduction gets `begin_schooljaar` and `einde_schooljaar` through `source.regulation`, with `parameters: {datum: $eerste_verlofdag}`.

### 2. What counts as "already granted"

- A decision under art. 13a with `verlof_verleend: true`. A refusal does not count.
- For the same child: `$besluit.bsn_jongere` equals the parameter `$bsn_jongere`. With `as`, the row is read only as `$besluit.<field>` ([RFC-049](/rfcs/rfc-049)).
- With its first leave day in the school year of the first leave day asked. Leave across two school years counts for the first (an own choice).
- A school year runs from 1 August to 31 July. The Leerplichtwet does not define it. The Wet op het primair onderwijs (art. 1) uses the same period but is not in the corpus, so the policy has no `legal_basis`.

### 3. What the cell does

`Cell::with_reductions` reads the reductions from the cell directory, one version per file at `reducties/<id>/<valid_from>.yaml`. It refuses rulework of another kind, and a reduction outside the directory of its `$id`. `Cell::decide` then calls `reading::bind`, which:

- loads the reductions into the service and unloads them after the decision (the `Bound` guard);
- finds, per input of the decision, the reduction in force that yields it;
- answers the `decisions` input of each such reduction with the chronicle's rows, scoped to that reduction;
- refuses, before anything runs: two reductions for one name, another type, a name the application, the policy, the cell or the head also supplies, a source that already answers the decisions, and a law already loaded under a reduction's `$id`.

`Bound::answer` runs the reductions with the decision's inputs and returns their answers. A parameter of a reduction that the decision does not hold is refused.

## Why

### Benefits

- The law and the policy hold no chronolex knowledge. Art. 13a asks a yes or no; the policy states a school year. A jurist reads both without knowing what a cell is.
- The rule of the school year lives once, in policy, and the reduction calls it like any article calls another.
- Any supplier can give the answer: a BDD scenario passes `verlof_al_verleend_in_schooljaar` as a parameter, the head without a cell as a value it supplies.
- One rule language. The reduction is type-checked, traced and tested like an article.

### Tradeoffs

- The link from reduction to decision is a name. A shared name by accident joins them; the type check and the refusal of a second supplier catch only part of that.
- Calling an article per FOREACH element is not possible yet. The reduction asks the policy once, for the asked leave, and compares each decision's first leave day with the dates it gets back.
- The first lesdag comes from the policy in force on the day of the decision, the school year from the first leave day.
- Only the directory keeps a reduction out of the corpus. Nothing yet refuses a `REDUCTIE` under `corpus/regulation` or created in the editor.

### Alternatives Considered

**The reduction yields a list, and a policy fills an open term from it** (the previous version of this RFC). The policy then reasoned about granted leave and the cell registered the list for it. The policy held logic that only exists because of the chronicle, and art. 13a needed an open term for what is a plain fact.

**The policy reads the decisions, and the cell recognizes its list by shape** (the first version). That leaked chronolex knowledge into the policy.

**A `registers:` binding in cell configuration** (PR #1689). The owner keeps cell configuration as a last resort.

**A reduction language in cell configuration** (RFC-022 §4.1 sketch). It repeats FOREACH without a type checker, trace or conformance suite.

## Implementation

- Schema v0.8.2: `regulatory_layer: REDUCTIE` and `source.decisions`, with the layer rules.
- Engine: `Source::decisions`; a `decisions` input resolves through a data source only; `load_check::reject_misplaced_reduction`.
- Corpus: art. 13a with the parameter `verlof_al_verleend_in_schooljaar`; the school-year policy with a scenario; the reduction under `corpus/cells/obs_de_klimop/`, validated by `just validate` and loaded only by the cell.
- `packages/cel`: `Reduction`, `reading::bind`, `Bound::answer`, `Cell::with_reductions`.
- Not built: calling an article per FOREACH element, which chronolexograms an answer read (RFC-022 §4.1 result listing), a reduction across cells.
